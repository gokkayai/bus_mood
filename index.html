A quick reality check.

London uses live arrival predictions from Transport for London. Predictions can change; this is not a guarantee you’ll catch your bus.

Berlin, New York, Tokyo and Istanbul are interactive demos with fictional departures. London also has a demo switch. Demo data is always labeled.

TfL arrival predictions do not provide a scheduled-time comparison here, so we never call a live bus “early” or “late.” Those scenarios are available in demo mode.

Departure advice uses uncertain bus arrivals and walking times. It picks the latest departure whose estimated probability of catching the bus meets your selected target under an independent normal-error model. These probabilities depend on the assumptions you choose and are not guarantees. “RUN!” is a joke, not a reason to cross a road unsafely.

This app is not affiliated with Google Maps or any transit agency.
