A quick reality check.

Berlin journey options come from vbb.transport.rest, a community-operated interface for VBB transport data. It is free to use but its availability is controlled by a third party.

The locations you enter are sent directly from this browser to that service. Search results are cached only for this browser tab’s session. Demo mode remains available and is always labeled.

VBB responses may mix realtime predictions with scheduled fallback. Every journey is labeled so scheduled information is not presented as live data.

Departure advice uses uncertain bus arrivals and walking times. It picks the latest departure whose estimated probability of catching the bus meets your selected target under an independent normal-error model. These probabilities depend on the assumptions you choose and are not guarantees. “RUN!” is a joke, not a reason to cross a road unsafely.

Data attribution: Verkehrsverbund Berlin-Brandenburg (VBB). This app is not affiliated with VBB or transport.rest.
